# OHS - Laptop Ergonomics Reflection

## Research Summary

### Risks of laptop-only use
- The screen sits too low, forcing the neck to bend downward and causing neck and shoulder strain
- The built-in keyboard angle forces wrists into an unnatural position, leading to wrist and forearm discomfort
- Prolonged use of a small screen at close range increases eye strain and headaches
- Poor posture over time can lead to chronic back pain and repetitive strain injuries

### Ergonomic equipment options
- Laptop stand to raise the screen to eye level
- External mouse and keyboard to allow natural arm positioning
- Ergonomic chair with lumbar support to maintain a neutral spine
- Monitor riser or stack of books as a low-cost alternative to a stand

### Ideal workspace setup
- Screen should be at eye level so your neck stays neutral (not tilted down or up)
- Chair height adjusted so feet are flat on the floor and knees at 90 degrees
- Elbows should be at desk level when typing, around 90 degrees bent
- Keep the screen about an arm's length away (50–70 cm)

### Daily habits to reduce strain
- Follow the 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to reduce eye strain
- Take a short movement break every 45–60 minutes to stand, stretch, or walk
- Do regular posture checks — sit upright, shoulders relaxed, back supported
- Avoid working with the laptop on your lap or on a low coffee table

## Reflection

### What equipment changes can I make?
I already have an external mouse, which immediately reduces the strain of using the trackpad all day and allows my hand and wrist to sit in a more natural position. I can also elevate my laptop using books or a stand to bring the screen closer to eye level and reduce neck strain.

### What behavioural changes will I implement?
- Sit upright with my lower back supported against the chair
- Keep my shoulders relaxed and not hunched forward
- Take a movement break every 45–60 minutes — stand up, stretch, walk around briefly
- Apply the 20-20-20 rule throughout the day to protect my eyes

### How will I remind myself?
I will use Focus Bear's built-in movement break and posture reminders to prompt me at regular intervals throughout the workday so these habits become automatic rather than something I have to remember manually.

## Workspace Change Made Today

I paired my external mouse with my laptop, which reduced wrist strain from trackpad use straight away. I also adjusted my sitting position so my back is supported and my screen is more at eye level. Going forward I plan to source a laptop stand to make the screen height adjustment permanent.
