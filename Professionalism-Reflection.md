# Professionalism at Focus Bear - Reflection

## Research Summary

### Professional vs Unprofessional Behaviour in a Casual Environment

| Situation | Professional | Unprofessional |
|---|---|---|
| Communication | Clear, respectful, timely replies | Leaving messages on read, vague responses |
| Meetings | Prepared, on time, engaged | Distracted, unprepared, talking over others |
| Feedback | Constructive, specific, kind | Personal, dismissive, defensive |
| Teamwork | Accountable, collaborative | Blame-shifting, unresponsive |
| Tone in messages | Friendly but clear | Passive aggressive or too casual |

### Respectful Communication in Remote Teams
- Assume good intent — text lacks tone, so don't read into messages negatively
- Be clear and concise — don't leave people guessing what you need
- Respond in a reasonable timeframe, even if just to say "on it"
- Disagree respectfully — focus on the idea, not the person
- Use inclusive language and be mindful of different backgrounds and time zones

### Giving and Receiving Feedback Professionally
- **Giving:** Focus on actions and outcomes, not personality. Be specific. 
  Say "this section could be clearer" not "this is confusing"
- **Receiving:** Listen without interrupting. Ask clarifying questions. 
  Avoid getting defensive. Say "thanks, I'll work on that"
- Always assume the other person wants you to succeed

## Reflection

### Great teamwork I have witnessed
During my university group projects and volunteering at Sant Nirankari Mission,
I observed that the most effective teams communicated openly, divided
responsibilities clearly, and checked in with each other regularly. What made
it work was mutual respect and accountability — people did what they said
they would do, and when they couldn't, they communicated early.

### How I will ensure clear, respectful, and inclusive communication
- Re-read messages before sending to check tone
- Be specific about what I need and by when
- Ask questions rather than making assumptions
- Use inclusive language and be mindful of how I phrase things

### How I can help create a positive work environment
- Bring a positive attitude to team interactions
- Acknowledge others' contributions
- Be approachable and willing to help
- Keep things light and have fun while staying accountable

### How I will prepare for meetings with supervisors
- Review any relevant materials or context beforehand
- Prepare specific questions or updates
- Take notes during the meeting
- Follow up with a summary or action items afterwards

### What it looks like to be proactive during my internship
- Not waiting to be told what to do next — checking in and asking
- Flagging blockers early rather than going silent
- Suggesting improvements or asking questions on issues rather than 
  just completing them on the surface
- Treating each task as an opportunity to learn and demonstrate capability

### How I will follow up if I need information or action from someone
1. **First:** Send a clear, polite message with context and a specific ask
2. **If no response in a reasonable time:** Follow up once with a gentle nudge
3. **If still no response:** Flag it to my supervisor so they are aware
4. **Escalate further** only if the blocker is affecting my ability to progress 
   on important work — and do so respectfully, not as a complaint

### Things I will not do
- Ghost colleagues or leave messages unread without acknowledgment
- Speak negatively about teammates
- Submit work I don't understand or haven't tested
- Stay silent when I am stuck or confused
- Interrupt or talk over people in meetings
- Take feedback personally or react defensively
