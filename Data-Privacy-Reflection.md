# Data Privacy & Confidentiality Reflection

## Research Summary

### Key Takeaways from Focus Bear's Privacy Policy

- Focus Bear is an Australian company (Focus Bear Pty Ltd) that complies with GDPR
- They collect several types of data: identification data, social/lifestyle data, 
  professional data, technical data, financial data, and usage data
- Habit data is **double encrypted** — meaning even Focus Bear staff cannot see it 
  unless a user reports a problem
- Payment details are handled by Stripe and Paddle — Focus Bear does not store 
  card details themselves
- Data is only retained as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected
- Third party processors include AWS, Auth0, PostHog, OpenAI, Stripe, Discord, 
  and Zoom — all operating under GDPR or equivalent frameworks
- Users can contact privacy@focusbear.io to exercise their rights (access, 
  correction, deletion, etc.)

### Types of Confidential Data at Focus Bear
- User personal information (email, occupation, device data)
- Habit and routine data (double encrypted)
- Payment and subscription data
- Internal company information and communications
- User survey responses and feedback

### Best Practices for Handling Confidential Data
- Only access data you need for your specific task (principle of least privilege)
- Never share user data outside authorised channels or with unauthorised people
- Do not store sensitive information in personal devices, emails, or unsecured locations
- Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication
- Lock your screen when stepping away from your computer
- Communicate internally using approved tools (e.g. Discord, Zoom)

### How to Respond to a Suspected Data Breach
- Do not try to handle it alone — report it immediately to your supervisor or team lead
- Document what happened: what data was involved, how it occurred, when you noticed it
- Under GDPR, data breaches must be reported to the relevant authority within 72 hours
- Focus Bear has a dedicated Security Breach Management process in place for this

## 📝 Reflection

### What steps can I take to handle data securely in my daily tasks?
As a data analytics intern, I will have access to user data and internal systems. 
I will only access data that is relevant to my assigned tasks, avoid downloading 
or storing data locally, and always use Focus Bear's approved tools for 
communication. I will also make sure my laptop is locked when I step away.

### How should I store, share, and dispose of sensitive information safely?
- Store: Only in authorised, encrypted systems — not in personal drives or emails
- Share: Only through approved internal channels with people who need access
- Dispose: Delete any temporary copies of sensitive data after use and confirm 
  deletion — do not leave files sitting in downloads or trash

### Common mistakes that lead to data privacy issues
- Sending sensitive data over personal email or messaging apps
- Using weak or reused passwords
- Leaving screens unlocked in public or shared spaces
- Accidentally sharing files with the wrong person
- Storing user data in unsecured spreadsheets or local files

---

## Security Measure I Will Implement

I will enable **two-factor authentication (2FA)** on my GitHub account and any 
other Focus Bear tools I am given access to. This is a simple but effective 
measure that significantly reduces the risk of unauthorised access, even if 
my password is compromised.

I will also make a habit of locking my screen whenever I step away from my workstation, even briefly.
