# 🐻 Company Reflection - Focus Bear

## Why was Focus Bear created?
Focus Bear was created to help people who struggle with executive functioning, 
particularly those with ADHD and Autism to build consistent daily habits and 
stay focused on what matters. The app was born out of a real need for a tool 
that goes beyond simple to-do lists and actually helps people get started, 
stay on track, and avoid distractions.

## What problem is Focus Bear solving?
Many productivity tools are designed for neurotypical users and assume that 
motivation, focus, and task initiation come naturally. For people with ADHD 
or Autism, these things can be genuinely difficult, not because of laziness 
or lack of effort, but because of how their brains are wired. Focus Bear 
solves this by combining habit tracking, distraction blocking, movement 
reminders, and morning routines into one supportive tool built specifically 
for neurodivergent users.

## Why is this mission important?
I have a distant cousin who has ADHD, and even from a distance I can see how 
much daily life can feel overwhelming when your brain works differently. Tools 
that are actually designed with those challenges in mind, rather than just 
expecting everyone to figure it out can make a real difference. Even for 
people without a diagnosis, having something that keeps you on track matters. 
I personally get overwhelmed when I have a lot on my plate, and I know from 
experience that having structure and the right tools genuinely helps with 
productivity and focus. Focus Bear addresses something real.

## How does Focus Bear align with my personal values?
I pursued a career in technology because I believe it makes lives easier and 
has no ceiling, there is always something new, always a problem to solve, 
always a way to improve how people live and work. What excites me most about 
tech is that it constantly surprises you. If you think about it in detail, 
the possibilities are endless and imagination is the only real limit. Working 
at a company like Focus Bear, where technology is being used to genuinely 
support people who need it, fits exactly into why I wanted to be in this 
industry in the first place.

## Do I personally relate to the challenges Focus Bear solves?
I do not have ADHD or Autism myself, but I relate to the feeling of being 
overwhelmed when too many things are happening at once. In those moments, 
having something that helps you slow down, prioritise, and just get started 
on one thing at a time is incredibly valuable. Focus Bear is essentially 
that, a tool that removes the friction between intention and action. I think 
a lot of people, diagnosed or not, could benefit from that kind of support.
