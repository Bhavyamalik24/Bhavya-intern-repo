# Learning Goals & Contributions - Bhavya Malik

## What do I hope to learn from this internship?
I hope to learn what it actually looks like to work with data in a real 
professional environment. At university, projects are small scale, the 
datasets are limited, the scope is defined, and nothing is particularly 
vast or ambiguous. What I am genuinely curious about is how data works 
when it is real, messy, and large. How do you handle it? How do you make 
sense of it? How do decisions get made from it? That gap between university 
projects and real-world data is what I am here to close.

I am also here to learn from people who have built something from scratch. 
There is something really valuable about being in a room with someone who took 
an idea and turned it into a product that real people 
use every day. That kind of experience cannot be taught in a classroom.

## What skills am I most excited to develop?
I am most excited to develop my data analytics skills in a live environment, 
working with real datasets, real tools, and real constraints. I want to 
understand how data flows through a product like Focus Bear, how it is 
collected, stored, and used to make decisions. I am also excited to improve 
how I communicate findings and work collaboratively with developers and 
stakeholders.

## What can I contribute to the team?
I can contribute consistency, a willingness to learn, and a fresh perspective. 
As a recent graduate I come without bad habits. I am moldable, open to 
feedback, and genuinely eager to absorb everything I can. I will say yes 
to things, try things I have not done before, and put in the effort to 
figure things out rather than waiting to be told exactly what to do. 
My current skills in data, documentation, and research also mean I can 
contribute meaningfully from day one while continuing to grow.

## What area do I feel least confident in?
Coding. I am honest about this. My programming skills are still developing 
and I know there will be moments where I feel out of my depth technically. 
But I see that as the point, this internship is exactly the environment 
where I want to build that confidence with real guidance and real problems 
to solve.

## What does success look like for me?
Success means finishing this internship having genuinely grown, not just 
ticking boxes, but actually understanding things I did not understand before. 
It means having contributed something real to the Focus Bear team, even in 
a small way. And it means walking away with skills, experience, and a 
clearer sense of where I want to take my career.

Learning never really ends and I think you should say yes to as many 
experiences as you can, there is always something to take away from them. 
That is the mindset I am bringing into this.
