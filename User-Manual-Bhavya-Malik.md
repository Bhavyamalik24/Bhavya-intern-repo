# My User Manual

## My Role
- I am working as a Data Analytics Intern at Focus Bear
- The days and times I work are: Monday, Tuesday and Friday.

---

## How I Work Best
- I do my best work when I have clear instructions and understand the "why" 
  behind a task — context helps me produce better output
- I struggle when expectations are vague or I am left without direction for 
  long periods, especially early in a project

---

## Communication Style
- I prefer messages to be clear and direct — tell me what you need and by when
- The best way to reach me is via Discord or email
- If you need something urgent, send me a direct message on SMS and I will 
  prioritise it

---

## Collaboration & Teamwork
- I work best in a team when roles are clear and people communicate openly 
  about blockers rather than going silent
- My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is when important updates are not shared 
  and people find out too late to adjust
- If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means I am listening carefully and 
  processing — I will speak up when I have something useful to add

---

## Focus & Productivity
- I stay focused by breaking tasks into smaller steps and working through them 
  one at a time
- Things that distract me include unclear priorities and context-switching 
  between too many tasks at once
- The best way to support my productivity is to give me a clear starting point 
  and check in periodically rather than leaving me completely without direction

---

## Feedback & Growth
- I like feedback to be direct and specific — tell me exactly what could be 
  improved and why, I won't take it personally
- When I make a mistake, the best way to let me know is privately and with 
  context so I can understand and fix it properly
- I appreciate it when teammates acknowledge effort as well as outcomes, 
  especially while I am still learning

---

## Fun Facts & Quirks
- One thing people often misunderstand about me is that my quietness in 
  unfamiliar situations is not disinterest — I am observing and getting 
  comfortable before I open up
- A fun fact about me: I do technical volunteer work at my local community 
  organisation outside of work
- My work superpower is: I pick up new tools and concepts quickly once I 
  understand the purpose behind them
